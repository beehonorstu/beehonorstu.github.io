## Information Collection and Use

### 1. Personal Data
We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number). Our applications do not require user account registration or login.

### 2. Location Data (On-Device Processing Only)
Certain features in our applications (such as the pass prediction feature in **ISS Tracker**) require access to your device's location (approximate or precise) to calculate and display the satellite's next visible pass over your current position.
- Location data is processed strictly in-memory on your device (ephemeral processing).
- We **never** upload, transmit, or store your location on any remote server.
- We **never** share or sell your location data to any third party or advertiser.

### 3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)
To support our free services, our applications may display advertisements powered by **Google AdMob**. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain device information to serve ads, prevent fraud, and analyze ad performance. This may include:
- **Device or other IDs:** such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID) or other device identifiers.
- **App Activity & Interactions:** user interactions with advertisements (views, impressions, and clicks).
- **App Performance & Diagnostics:** diagnostic and error logs, ad loading latency, and performance data.

All data transmitted by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS protocols. For more information about how Google collects and processes user data, please review:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
