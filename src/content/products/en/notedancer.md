## Make every note dance

**[NoteDancer](https://notedancer.com/)** is a browser-based MIDI visualizer created for people who want to see music as well as hear it. Select a classical demo or import your own MIDI file, and watch the generated musical staves move as each note plays.

The picture above is a real screenshot of NoteDancer visualizing Beethoven's *Für Elise*.

## What you can do

- **Bring a MIDI file.** Import a `.mid` or `.midi` file of up to 15 MB, or start with Bach's *Prelude in C Major*, Beethoven's *Für Elise*, or Mozart's *Turkish March*.
- **Watch and listen together.** Two glowing guides hop between notes on the generated treble and bass staves, synchronized with sampled grand piano audio.
- **Choose the pace.** Play at 0.5× to 2× speed without changing piano pitch.
- **Export the performance.** Create a synchronized **720p H.264/AAC MP4** for portrait (9:16) or landscape (16:9) on compatible browsers, including the original piano audio.
- **Keep your MIDI private.** MIDI parsing, music playback and video encoding run locally in your browser. No account is required.

NoteDancer generates an approximate visual score from MIDI note events; it is not a replacement for professionally engraved sheet music.
