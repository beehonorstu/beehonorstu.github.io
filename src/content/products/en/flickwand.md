✨ **Flick Wand** — Unleash the Magic of Your Gestures

Flick Wand reimagines how you interact with your phone. Instead of ordinary taps and swipes, control your device with unique hand gestures, bringing a touch of magic to every interaction.

## 🪄 Key Features

- **Draw your gesture to awaken apps** 📱
- **Conjure websites with a flick** 🌐
- **Dial a call effortlessly** 📞
- **Chat with your friends instantly** 💬

## 🎩 Why Flick Wand?

- **Personalized Magic** — Create custom gestures for actions that matter most to you.
- **Intuitive and Time-Saving** — Just flick your finger, and watch it happen instantly.
- **Whimsical Fun** — You're the wizard, and your phone obeys your spells.

## 🪄 How it Works

1. Define your magic gestures
2. Assign them to tasks
3. Cast your spells anytime!

Flick Wand is designed around custom gestures that you assign to everyday actions. Gesture definitions are stored locally in the app.

## Common questions about Flick Wand

### What can a custom gesture do?

You can draw a gesture and associate it with an action, such as launching an app or opening a website. Supported actions can depend on the permissions and capabilities of your Android device.

### Is Flick Wand a launcher replacement?

Flick Wand is a gesture shortcut utility. It helps you reach specific actions quickly rather than replacing every part of the Android home screen.
