🛰️ **ISS Tracker** — Track the ISS real-time, get precision pass predictions, and watch live video.

ISS Tracker is an Android application for tracking the International Space Station (ISS) in real time.

## Core Features

- 🗺️ **Real-time orbital tracking** — Interactive map displaying the current location and orbit of the ISS.
- 📊 **Visible pass predictions** — Calculated based on your local time, elevation, and eclipse conditions.
- 📺 **Live HD video streams** — Watch cameras aboard the ISS in real time.
- 📡 **Real-time telemetry data** — Altitude, speed, latitude, and longitude at your fingertips.

## Common questions about ISS Tracker

### Can I see where the ISS is right now?

Yes. ISS Tracker displays the station's current orbital position on a map, with a 3D Earth view and telemetry such as altitude and speed. Orbital information is updated using online data sources.

### How can I know when the ISS will pass overhead?

Use the visible-pass prediction feature. Predictions use orbital information and your location to estimate upcoming passes; actual visibility also depends on local conditions, such as weather and lighting.

### Can I watch video from the space station?

The app provides access to ISS-related live video sources. Stream availability and camera content depend on the external providers and may change.

### Does ISS Tracker need location access or an internet connection?

Location permission is optional and is used for pass predictions. Core maps, online orbital data, imagery and live streams may require an internet connection.

## Privacy and transparency

Location access is optional and used for visible-pass predictions. The app stores orbital data and preferences locally, while its release version also uses Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics and retrieves space content online.
