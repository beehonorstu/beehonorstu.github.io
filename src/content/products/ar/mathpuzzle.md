**About Math Puzzle**

Math Puzzle is a number puzzle game that is easy to pick up and becomes more satisfying the more you play. Look at the cross equations on the board, choose the right numbers from the available options, and fill the empty spaces so every horizontal and vertical equation works.

The rules are simple and clear: spot the relationship between the numbers, work out the result and place the right number in the right position. Each round is a self-contained maths puzzle. There are no complicated controls or long rules to learn; just focus on the board in front of you and solve it step by step with calculation and logic.

Play at your own pace. When you want to relax, choose an easier difficulty and enjoy the satisfying rhythm of filling in numbers. When you want a sharper challenge, raise the difficulty and try more complex equation combinations and board shapes. You can customise the number range, operators, equation count and board style, so every round feels fresh.

Math Puzzle fits neatly into short breaks. Whether you are travelling, taking a pause or winding down before bed, it offers just the right amount of thoughtful fun. Each correct number makes the board a little clearer; when the whole board is complete, you get that clean, rewarding feeling of a puzzle solved.

**Game Features**

- Cross-equation puzzles that combine arithmetic and logical thinking
- Multiple difficulty levels for relaxed play or a more focused challenge
- Customisable number ranges, operators and board shapes
- A fresh layout in every round to keep the game feeling new
- Hints, undo and continue features for a smoother experience
- Warm, clean board design that is easy to read and ideal for a quick round anytime

If you enjoy number games, maths fill-in puzzles, logic puzzles or light brain-teasing challenges, Math Puzzle is a simple, comfortable and replayable choice. Fill in the numbers, connect the equations and complete your own maths puzzle.
